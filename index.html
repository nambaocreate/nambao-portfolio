INTRODUCTION

Nam Bảo is a graduate of Design Studies at RMIT Vietnam and one of the only two recipients of the Creativity Scholarship 2022.

Guided by a belief in finding the extraordinary in the ordinary, Bảo sees designers as artists – both sharing the ability to see romance in the minuscule, and elevating even the smallest details into something worth remembering.
